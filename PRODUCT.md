# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Rai and developers tracking coding-agent usage across machines and paid Accounts.

## Product Purpose

Local multi-provider usage accounting. The dashboard prioritises remaining Account quotas so the user can see available capacity, then usage spend and subsidisation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Account is a provider login and its bill combined. Show short-term and weekly quotas for current and all Accounts. Active selection belongs to a machine and provider. Preserve local history and distinct Account fees across machines. No fabricated attribution, quota forecasts or summed quota percentages. Keep the local five-hour spend block; remove what-if. Explore holds usage breakdowns; Settings holds account and sync management.

## Brand Commitments

Keep chaching's Register & Receipt tokens, existing mark and personality. No new cost-disclosure copy or work/personal cutover controls. Layout, not branding, is under comparison.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed in the Account, subsidisation and dashboard IA decision sessions. Source inventory in the resolution of dashboard information architecture on the repository issue tracker. Prototype data is explicitly fictional and does not claim to show live usage.

## Dashboard copy

No decorative taglines or redundant introductory chrome. Use functional data labels. Retain existing cold-scan personality; do not invent slogans to decorate dashboard regions.
